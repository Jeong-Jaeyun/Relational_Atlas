\section*{Motivation and status}
In the completed stage-17 family, different-pair same-sign transfer had exact
$\Lambda=9.90$ but bootstrap $p=0.02640$, below nominal significance but above the
familywise 0.0125 threshold. This is a new, independent follow-up; its evidence is
not pooled with stage 17.

\section*{Frozen replication}
Use only source-return $(0,2)$, $n=3$, $P=0$, $\tau=0$, $\epsilon=\pi/2$, skewed
largest-remainder labels, workers 6, 262144 shots per uniform label, Aer seed
20261119, and 10000 bounded weak-null bootstraps. The doubled allocation predicts
$\Lambda\simeq19.79$. The sole primary decision threshold is 0.05.

\section*{Resources and integrity}
The run uses $2\times3\times7\times262144=11{,}010{,}048$ preparations. Raw counts
are checkpointed before analysis and must satisfy joint-all-zero count
$\leq$ clock-acceptance count.
