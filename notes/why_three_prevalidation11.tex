\section*{Conclusion}
Both predeclared positives pass the familywise criterion; the exact-null uniform
control and subthreshold condition do not. The paired $\epsilon=1.0$ constraint
condition has observed pooled differences $(1.309,0.713,0.064)\times10^{-3}$ and
is detected without treating the exact Aer prediction as data. The pattern confirms
the mechanism and predicted power boundary for this toy circuit. It does
\emph{not} establish spontaneous physical dimension selection or justify a general
theory claim.
