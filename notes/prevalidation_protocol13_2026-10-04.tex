\section*{Question}
Stage 12 found leakage only for a paired transverse operation with the same qubit
support, but it used qubit zero for the positive example. This protocol asks whether
the rule holds for every system qubit and whether it fails for every alternative
return support from qubit zero.

\section*{Frozen conditions}
At $\epsilon=1$ rad, evaluate paired inverse $R_x$ on each same-support pair
$(q,q)$ for $q=0,\ldots,5$, then the five cross-support controls $(0,q)$ for
$q=1,\ldots,5$. Each row evaluates all blocks, axes, labels, physical conditions,
and both frozen clock phases exactly with workers 6. The position remains directly
after preparation and directly after the science phase layer.

\section*{Decision discipline}
This is an exact-Aer mechanism map, not a significance experiment. The principal
outcome is the range of $P=0$, $\tau=0$ constraint contrasts over same supports and
the maximum over cross supports. A later finite-shot study must predeclare a new
sampling criterion after these results are fixed.
