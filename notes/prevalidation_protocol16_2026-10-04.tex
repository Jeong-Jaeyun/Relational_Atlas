\section*{Question}
Stages 14--15 map support and dimension dependence at one pulse angle. This protocol
tests whether each of the four $n=3$ support classes has a common transverse
amplitude dependence, including the expected periodic endpoint at $\epsilon=\pi$.

\section*{Frozen exact-Aer grid}
At $n=3$, use the stage-14 representatives $(0,0)$, $(0,1)$, $(0,2)$, and $(0,3)$,
skewed largest-remainder label weights, all axes, both phase blocks, and
$\epsilon\in\{0.1,0.2,0.5,0.8,1.0,1.2,\pi/2,2.0,\pi\}$. The operation remains
$R_x(+\epsilon)$ after preparation and $R_x(-\epsilon)$ after the science phase
layer.

\section*{Recorded diagnostics}
For every row record the maximum $P=0$ pooled contrast, its three axis components,
clock-acceptance error, and $\sin^2\epsilon$. After all rows are completed, report
the range of contrast divided by $\sin^2\epsilon$ away from the endpoint and the
contrast at $\epsilon=\pi$. No finite-shot decision is made.
