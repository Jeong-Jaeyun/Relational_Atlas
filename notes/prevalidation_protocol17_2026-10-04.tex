\section*{Frozen family}
At $n=3$, $\epsilon=\pi/2$, skewed largest-remainder labels, $P=0$, $\tau=0$,
workers 6, 131072 shots per uniform label, Aer seed 20261117, and 10000 bounded
weak-null bootstraps, test four source-return rows: same support $(0,0)$; conjugate
partner $(0,1)$; different-pair same-sign $(0,2)$; and different-pair opposite-sign
$(0,3)$. The familywise Bonferroni threshold is $0.0125$.

\section*{Pre-execution expectations}
Exact Aer predicts approximate noncentralities 33.80, numerical zero, 9.90, and
27.61 in this order at the frozen budget. Thus the three nonzero classes are
predeclared positives, with the same-sign class close to the design screen; the
conjugate partner is the exact-null control. Exact probabilities are reported but
do not replace the finite-shot decision.
