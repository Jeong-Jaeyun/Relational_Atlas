\section*{Exact matrix summary}
The full source-to-return matrix is symmetric to $1.6\times10^{-16}$. Its entries
fall into four exact-Aer classes:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lr}
\toprule
support relationship & maximum $P=0$, $\tau=0$ contrast\\
\midrule
same support & $1.4421688575454\times10^{-3}$\\
conjugate partner in the same momentum pair & at most $1.6\times10^{-16}$\\
different momentum pair, matching sign bit & $3.876167517154\times10^{-4}$\\
different momentum pair, opposite sign bit & $6.978119628691\times10^{-4}$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The three conjugate pairs are $(0,1)$, $(2,3)$, and $(4,5)$. All six diagonal
entries agree to numerical precision; all twelve entries in each off-pair class do
as well.

\section*{Interpretation}
The paired transverse intervention obeys an encoding-level transfer pattern, not a
same-qubit rule. Return to the conjugate partner cancels the constraint contrast,
whereas transfer to another momentum pair remains observable and depends on the
relative sign-bit class. This explains both the stage-12 zero q0-to-q1 control and
the stage-13 counterexamples without changing any sampling criterion after the fact.

\section*{Scope}
The matrix is exact Aer evidence for this deliberately constructed $n=3$ circuit.
It motivates testing the selection pattern under a changed number of momentum pairs;
it does not establish a general physical selection law.
