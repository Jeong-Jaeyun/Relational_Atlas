\section*{Frozen design screen}
Stage 9 showed the leakage mechanism but not an experimentally useful amplitude.
Before any finite-shot run, this exact Aer scan fixed two screens: structural
separation $D_0/D_{\mathrm{strong}}\geq0.1$ and approximate finite-shot constraint
noncentrality $\Lambda_C\geq10$ under the stage-6 allocation.

\section*{Exact Aer boundary}
Structural separation first exceeds $0.1$ at $\epsilon=0.2$ radians:
$D_0=8.04\times10^{-5}$, $D_{\mathrm{strong}}=7.44\times10^{-4}$, ratio $0.108$.
This point has only $\Lambda_C=0.034$, and is deliberately below finite-shot reach.
The detection screen is crossed first at $\epsilon=1.0$ radians, where
$D_0=1.442\times10^{-3}$, $D_{\mathrm{strong}}=8.767\times10^{-4}$, ratio $1.645$,
and $\Lambda_C=14.54$.

\section*{Interpretation}
There are two distinct thresholds: a smaller amplitude makes null leakage visible
in exact probabilities; a larger amplitude is needed for this finite-shot decision
procedure. This distinction fixed, rather than tuned after observing, the two
amplitudes used in stage 11.
